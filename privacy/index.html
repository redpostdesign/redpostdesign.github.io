Privacy Policy

Effective 1 October 2026 · Red Post Design

Red Post Design is an independent poster shop selling original art prints and digital downloads through Etsy. This policy explains what information we handle through this website and through the Red Post Design Pinterest app, which we use to publish our own product images to our own Pinterest account.

The Red Post Design Pinterest app

The app is an internal tool. It is used only by Red Post Design, on Red Post Design's own Pinterest account, and it is not offered to anyone else. With the permissions we grant it through Pinterest's authorization screen, it may:

  • read our account's basic profile and our list of boards, so it can choose where a pin goes;
  • create pins of our own poster designs on our own boards;
  • read engagement statistics for our own pins, so we can see which designs people like.

The app does not read, collect or store information about other Pinterest users, including the people who view, save or click our pins. It does not follow, message or interact with other accounts.

How we store it

The access token Pinterest issues to the app is kept on our own computer, outside any public code, and is never shared or sold. Pin statistics are kept only in our private shop records to guide which designs we make next. We keep information only as long as we need it for that purpose. Access can be revoked at any time from the Pinterest account's settings (Security and logins › Apps), which makes the token unusable immediately.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share information obtained from Pinterest with anyone. We do not use it for advertising profiles and we do not combine it with other data about individuals.

This website

This site uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking, and it has no forms, so it collects no personal information. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log basic technical data such as IP addresses for security; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. The page loads its display font from Google Fonts; see Google's Privacy Policy.

Orders

Purchases happen on Etsy, not on this site. Order and payment details are handled under Etsy's Privacy Policy and our shop policies on Etsy. We use an order's details only to fulfil it, including sending digital download links.

Children

This site and the app are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Your choices

If you believe we hold information about you and want it corrected or deleted, contact us and we will act on it promptly.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.

Contact

Message us through our Etsy shop: open etsy.com/shop/RedPostDesign and use the Message button.
